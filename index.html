Coodle Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 24, 2026

Coodle helps you see which of your credit cards earns the most for a purchase. This policy explains what the Coodle app sends to our server, why, and how long it is kept. Coodle has no accounts, shows no ads, uses no analytics or tracking tools, and does not sell your data.

What stays on your device

Your wallet (card names, issuers and nicknames), your recent questions and a random install ID are stored on your device. You can remove cards and recent questions in the app. Deleting the app removes the data it stored. Coodle never asks for card numbers, security codes (CVV) or expiry dates.

What the app sends to us

  • Card searches: the text you type to find a card.
  • Questions: your purchase description, the optional amount, the date on your device, and the cards in your wallet (catalog cards, or a card name and issuer). We use these to rank your cards.
  • Install ID and IP address: used only to limit how many requests can be made. We do not write them to our logs. They are stored only briefly, as keyed one-way hashes.

Third-party processing

To work out what kind of purchase you described, your purchase text may be sent to Anthropic, a provider of AI models. We do not send Anthropic your install ID, your IP address or your wallet. Please do not type personal or sensitive information in the purchase description.

What we keep, and for how long

  • Request records: the purchase text, its category and merchant, the number of cards, and technical timing details, with no install ID, IP address or card list. Kept for 90 days.
  • Saved classifications: purchase text, its category, merchant and any amount mentioned in the text, kept so that the same question does not need to be classified again. It carries no user identifier and has no fixed expiry.
  • Card name counts: for cards that are not in our catalog, an anonymous count of each card name and issuer, to decide which cards to add. Removed 180 days after the name was last seen.
  • "Not helpful" feedback: only if you send it, we store that question (purchase text, amount and card list), the results you were shown, and any comment you typed. It has no install ID or IP address. Kept for 180 days.

Coodle staff may read stored purchase texts and feedback to correct classifications and improve results. We mask long digit sequences that look like card numbers in stored purchase text and comments.

Sharing

We do not sell or rent your data, or use it to track you across apps and websites. Data is processed by the service providers that run Coodle (including server hosting and Anthropic), under their terms of service, and may be disclosed if the law requires it. We share data with them only so that they can provide their service to us, and expect them to protect it at least as described in this policy.

Your choices

Sending feedback is optional. Because stored records carry no user identifier, we usually cannot tell which ones are yours. If you want us to look for and delete something you typed, email us with the text and the approximate date.

Children

Coodle is not directed to children under 13.
